Griha Pravesh Puja

Naye ghar mein pehla kadam shubh vidhi ke saath.

Ye puja kya hai

Naye ghar mein rehna shuru karne se pehle ki jaane wali puja Griha Pravesh kehlati hai. Isme Ganesh pujan, kalash sthapana, vastu shanti aur havan kiya jata hai, aur doodh ubalne ki rasm bhi hoti hai.

Puja mein kya hota hai

  • Ganesh pujan aur kalash sthapana
  • Navgrah aur vastu devta pujan
  • Havan
  • Rasoi mein doodh ubalna aur aarti

Zaroori samagri

Kalash, nariyal, aam ke patte, roli, moli, akshat, haldi, phool, ghee, havan samagri, mithai aur doodh. Poori final suchi Pandit ji puja se pehle bhej denge.
